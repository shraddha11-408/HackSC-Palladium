# Palladium — pitch outline

1. Problem: video is difficult to monitor continuously; reviewers need a way to prioritize observable events with context.
2. Product: phone/upload input, temporal AI observations, preserved incident windows, human review.
3. Demo: pair a phone, show a staged clip, inspect evidence and uncertainty, record a review decision.
4. Technical design: one affordable NVIDIA GPU; realtime models first; heavy enhancement/3D jobs serialized after streams close.
5. Trust: anonymous labels, no demographic inference, original hashes, labeled derived artifacts, local decisions and action history.
6. Evidence: show measured latency, precision/recall and false alerts/hour only after a real inference evaluation. Identify simulated demonstrations explicitly.
7. Adoption: propose a consent-based, restricted pilot with authorized campus stakeholders; validate reviewer usefulness before expanding.

This is content for a future slide deck, not a finished presentation or a claim that a campus pilot exists.
